# Equipment

## Weapons

### Weapon Categories
Weapons determine damage die (volatility), accuracy modifier (reliability), and category (used for training and shield restrictions).

| Category | Main Hand Accuracy | Off Hand Accuracy | Damage Die | Penetration | Notes |
|----------|--------------------|-------------------|------------|-------------|-------|
| Simple   | +2                 | +0                | d4         | —           |       |
| Light    | +1                 | −3                | d6         | —           |       |
| Standard | 0                  | −6                | d8         | —           |       |
| Heavy    | −1                 | —                 | d10        | −2          | Two-handed unless trained |
| Brutal   | −2                 | —                 | d12        | −4          | Always two-handed; on a successful hit, applies a minor condition appropriate to the weapon (e.g. Bleeding for a greatsword, Prone or Staggered for a hammer). The condition is defined when the weapon is acquired and approved by the GM. |
| Unarmed  | +4                 | +3                | None       | —           | Does not use a weapon die |

Heavy and Brutal weapons have no off-hand value because they cannot be wielded in one hand (Heavy requires training to use single-handed).

**Penetration** reduces the target's effective mitigation for that attack. A Heavy weapon (Penetration −2) treats a target with 5 Physical Mitigation as if it has 3. Penetration cannot reduce effective mitigation below 0.

### Weapon Training (Class Modifier)
Each instance applies to one weapon category (must specify melee **or** ranged):
- +1 to attack rolls with that category
- +1 to damage with that category

May be selected multiple times to cover additional categories or stack bonuses within a single category.

### Dual Wielding
A character who chooses not to use a shield may wield:
- Any two-handed weapon, or
- Up to two one-handed weapons (one per hand, any categories, even mixed)

**Dual wielding characters automatically attack with both weapons each turn, unless the player specifies otherwise or circumstances require a different approach.**

Use the **main hand accuracy modifier** for the primary weapon and the **off hand accuracy modifier** for the secondary weapon.

## Armor

Armor adds a modifier to Physical Defense and a bonus to Physical Mitigation. It does not affect Mental Defense or Mental Mitigation. Only one armor may be worn at a time.

> **Design note:** The defense modifier represents encumbrance. Heavy armor makes you easier to hit but harder to wound. Being unarmored makes you harder to hit but offers no damage protection.

| Category | Defense Modifier | Mitigation Bonus |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| None     | +2              | +0              |
| Light    | +0              | +2              |
| Standard | −1              | +3              |
| Heavy    | −2              | +4              |
| Bulwark  | −4              | +6              |

### Armor Training (Class Modifier)
Each instance applies to one armor category:
- +1 to that armor's **defense modifier**
- +1 to that armor's **mitigation**

May be selected multiple times. With enough training, heavy armor can yield a net positive defense modifier.

## Shields

Shields are a separate defensive item from armor. A character may carry one shield at a time. Shields improve Physical Defense only.

Shields do not protect against mental attacks, with narrow exceptions at GM discretion (e.g., a target fully concealed behind a shield may avoid a mental attack that requires eye contact).

| Shield | Defense Bonus | Weapons Available |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| Small  | +1 Physical Defense | Any one-handed weapon. |
| Medium | +2 Physical Defense | Simple, Light, or Standard weapons. |
| Large  | +4 Physical Defense | Requires both hands; no weapon. With 2 ranks of Shield Training: may wield one-handed with a Simple or Light weapon. With 3 ranks: Simple, Light, or Standard weapons. |

### Shield Training (Class Modifier)
- Each instance grants +1 to Physical Defense when wielding any shield.
- **2 instances:** may wield a large shield in one hand, freeing the other hand for a Simple or Light weapon.
- **3 instances:** may also wield Standard weapons alongside a large shield.

May be selected multiple times. Unlocks at 2 and 3 instances apply only when those thresholds are reached.

## Charms

Charms function identically to armor but apply to **Mental Defense** and **Mental Mitigation** instead of physical. Only one charm may be worn at a time.

| Category | Defense Modifier | Mitigation Bonus |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| None     | +2              | +0              |
| Light    | +0              | +2              |
| Standard | −1              | +3              |
| Heavy    | −2              | +4              |
| Bulwark  | −4              | +6              |

### Charm Training (Class Modifier)
Each instance applies to one charm category:
- +1 to that charm's **defense modifier**
- +1 to that charm's **mitigation**

May be selected multiple times. With enough training, a heavy charm can yield a net positive mental defense modifier.
