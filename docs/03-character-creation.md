# Character Creation

Character creation follows these steps in order:

1. [Character Concept](#1-character-concept)
2. [Assign Traits](#2-assign-traits)
3. [Choose Species](#3-species)
4. [Define Class](#4-class)
5. [Choose Magic (or not)](#5-magic-optional)
6. [Select Specializations](#6-specializations)
7. [Calculate Derived Values](#7-derived-values)
8. [Choose Starting Equipment](#8-starting-equipment)

---

## 1. Character Concept

- Define who your character is narratively without assigning any mechanical attributes.
- Create an origin story for your character — who you are at level 1.
- Imagine your ultimate evolution — who you want to be at the end of the campaign.
- Establish your reason for adventuring.
- Each player is encouraged to create all of this in coordination with the other players, to establish pre-existing bonds among the characters.

---

## 2. Assign Traits
- Distribute **20 trait points** across the 12 traits.
- No more than **two traits** may begin at 4.
- No trait may begin **above 4**.
- Traits may begin at 0.

*(See [Traits](02-traits.md) for the full trait list and all formulas.)*

---

## 3. Species
- A player may choose any species.
- Species do not grant mechanical benefits at character creation.
- Exceptional species traits (wings, supernatural features, etc.) must be explained narratively and may not provide mechanical benefit at level 1.

---

## 4. Class
Classes are not pre-written archetypes. Players define their class narratively, then select **3 Class Mechanic Modifiers** from the list below.

Modifiers may be selected more than once — all stack freely. The 3-pick budget is the limiting factor.

### Class Mechanic Modifiers

| Modifier                  | Effect                                              |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Movement Speed            | +5 feet to combat speed                             |
| Physical Damage Mitigation| +1 to Physical Mitigation                           |
| Mental Damage Mitigation  | +1 to Mental Mitigation                             |
| Physical Damage           | +1 to physical damage                               |
| Magical Damage            | +1 to magical damage                                |
| Attack Bonus              | +1 to physical or magical attack rolls              |
| Resolve                   | +2 to Resolve                                       |
| Resonance                 | +1 to Resonance                                     |
| Additional Specialization | Gain 1 extra specialization                         |
| Weapon Training           | See below                                           |
| Armor Training            | See below                                           |
| Charm Training            | See below                                           |
| Shield Training           | See below                                           |

### Weapon Training
Each instance applies to one weapon category (must specify melee **or** ranged):
- +1 to attack rolls with that category
- +1 to damage with that category

May be selected multiple times to cover additional categories or stack bonuses within a single category.

### Armor Training
Each instance applies to one armor category:
- +1 to that armor's defense modifier
- +1 to that armor's mitigation

May be selected multiple times.

### Charm Training
Each instance applies to one charm category:
- +1 to that charm's defense modifier
- +1 to that charm's mitigation

May be selected multiple times.

### Shield Training
Each instance grants +1 to Physical Defense when wielding any shield. May be selected multiple times.

- **2 instances:** may wield a large shield in one hand, freeing the other hand for a Simple or Light weapon.
- **3 instances:** may also wield Standard weapons alongside a large shield.

---

## 5. Magic (Optional)
Choose whether your character uses magic.

### If Magical
- Choose a **Magical Domain** — a thematic description of the nature of your magic.
- Define **2 Foundational Effects** that:
  - Fall within the chosen domain
  - Are relatively minor
  - Are approved by the GM
  - Are narrow enough to prevent universal application, but broad enough to have multiple uses

Typically (but not required): one combat effect and one role-play effect.

*(See [Magic](08-magic.md) for full spellcasting and Resonance rules.)*

### If Non-Magical
- No magical domain.
- No foundational effects.
- Gain **4 additional specializations** instead.

---

## 6. Specializations
At level 1, gain:
- 1 Combat Specialization
- 1 Role-Play Specialization
- Plus any gained from class modifiers or the non-magical bonus above

*(See [Specializations](05-specializations.md) for full rules.)*

---

## 7. Derived Values
Calculate all derived statistics after assigning traits and applying modifiers:

- Physical Defense
- Mental Defense
- Physical Mitigation
- Mental Mitigation
- Resolve
- Resonance *(if magical)*
- Combat Speed
- Out-of-combat speed

*(See [Traits](02-traits.md) for all formulas.)*

---

## 8. Starting Equipment
A character may begin with:
- One weapon
- One armor *(optional)*
- One charm *(optional)*
- One shield *(optional)*
- Other accessories *(cosmetic unless otherwise stated)*

Restrictions:
- Only one armor may be worn at a time.
- Only one charm may be worn at a time.
- Shield choice may restrict weapon categories.

*(See [Equipment](07-equipment.md) for categories and rules.)*
