# Conditions

Conditions are outcomes, not recipes. Any condition may be applied through creative player action — there are no prescribed mechanics for how a condition is initiated. A player may attempt to Grapple, Blind, Stagger, Charm, or apply any other condition through any approach they can imaginatively justify. The GM determines whether a roll is required, the difficulty of the attempt, and which condition best represents the result.

This list is a reference framework, not an exhaustive catalogue. Players and GMs are free to create new conditions as the situation demands. The entries here provide a mechanical baseline and a starting point for improvisation.

---

## Physical Conditions

| Condition     | Effect |
|---------------|--------|
| **Bleeding**  | Takes physical damage at the beginning of each turn. |
| **Blinded**   | Cannot see. Cannot perform actions relying on sight. Disadvantage on actions where sight would help. |
| **Burning**   | Takes fire damage at the beginning of each turn. Fire damage does not automatically cause Burning unless stated. |
| **Chilled**   | Speed halved. −2 to Physical Defense. |
| **Dazed**     | Cannot perform Minor Adjustments. Speed halved. |
| **Deafened**  | Cannot hear. Cannot perform actions relying on hearing. Disadvantage on actions where hearing would help. |
| **Exhausted** | Speed halved. Can only move OR take a Major Action (not both). −2 to all attacks, defenses, and trait checks. |
| **Grappled**  | Cannot move unless they can also move the grappling creature. |
| **Immobilized** | Cannot move. |
| **Paralyzed** | Cannot move. Cannot take actions. Physical attacks against the character have advantage. |
| **Poisoned**  | −2 to all trait checks and attacks. |
| **Prone**     | Speed halved. Melee attacks against the character have advantage. |
| **Restrained**| Speed halved. Movement may be limited by the restraint's anchor. |
| **Slowed**    | Speed halved. −2 to Physical Defense. −2 to attack rolls. |
| **Staggered** | −2 to Mental Defense. |
| **Stunned**   | Cannot take actions. −2 to Mental Defense. |
| **Wounded**   | Used when a specific injury or vulnerability needs a mechanical hook. Effect is defined by whatever applied the condition. |

## Mental Conditions

| Condition     | Effect |
|---------------|--------|
| **Afraid**    | −2 to Mental Defense. |
| **Berserk**   | On their turn, must attack the closest character (friend or foe). If multiple are equidistant, determine randomly. |
| **Charmed**   | Treats the applier as a friend for all mechanical and narrative purposes. |
| **Confused**  | On their turn, acts randomly in a manner inconsistent with circumstances. Nature of randomness determined on that turn. |
| **Dominated** | The character's turn is controlled by the applier of the effect. |
| **Panicked**  | −2 to Mental Defense. −2 to all trait rolls. |
| **Shaken**    | −2 to Mental Mitigation. |
| **Silenced**  | Cannot speak. Cannot produce sound from the mouth. |
| **Stressed**  | −2 to Mental Defense. −2 to Mental Mitigation. |

---

## Magical Conditions

| Condition      | Effect |
|----------------|--------|
| **Cursed**     | Each curse is unique. Effects and removal conditions are defined when applied. Lasts until removal conditions are met unless stated otherwise. |
| **Hexed**      | Similar to a curse but temporary. By default lasts until the end of the target's next turn. |
| **Marked**     | No inherent effect. Enables additional effects, penalties, or bonuses as defined by the applier. |
| **Muted**      | Spell damage is halved. Conditions applied by spells fail. |
| **Possessed**  | Target's body is taken over by another soul. All abilities are channeled through the possessed body. Unless stated otherwise, the possessor's original body dies if left without a soul. |
| **Sealed**     | Cannot cast spells. |
| **Suppressed** | All Resonance costs are doubled. |
| **Weakened**   | All damage dealt (physical and mental) is halved. |

## Status Conditions

| Condition      | Effect |
|----------------|--------|
| **Distracted** | Attacks against the target have advantage. |
| **Exposed**    | Attacks against the target gain +2. |
| **Focused**    | +2 to a single task or action. −2 to Physical and Mental Defense against all other sources. |
| **Hidden**     | Cannot be directly targeted by actions. May still be affected by area effects. |
| **Surprised**  | Goes last in round order. Does not automatically act first in the following round. |
| **Vulnerable** | −2 to Physical and Mental Mitigation. |

---

## Recovery Conditions

| Condition       | Effect |
|-----------------|--------|
| **Dying**       | Applied when Resolve reaches 0. The character will die after one minute unless stabilized or healed. Each turn, the character makes a stabilization roll (d20 + Endurance + Willpower + current Resolve ≥ 14). If Resolve reaches the negative of its maximum value, death is instant regardless of the timer. |
| **Stabilized**  | Unconscious but not dying. |
| **Unconscious** | Cannot move. Cannot act. Cannot see or hear. Attacks against the creature have advantage. Any attack that beats a 7 (Very Easy) automatically hits. Mitigation from armor still applies unless the GM rules otherwise. |

---

## Ongoing Damage and Rest
If a character is suffering ongoing damage, they cannot Rest or Sleep for recovery. Instead, they may spend the rest or sleep period treating the condition — removing the ongoing damage without gaining any Resolve or Resonance recovery. This applies when the condition can reasonably be treated in the available time. The GM may rule that some conditions cannot be removed this way.
