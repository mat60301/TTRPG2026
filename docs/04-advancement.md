# Level Advancement

There is no level cap. Trait dice may never exceed d12 regardless of level.

---

## Odd Levels
*(1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, …)*

- Gain **+1 Trait Point**, assignable to any of the 12 traits.
- There is no upper limit to trait values after level 1.

---

## Even Levels
*(2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, …)*

- Upgrade **one Trait Die** by one step: d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
- Trait dice cannot exceed d12.

---

## Levels Divisible by 3
*(3, 6, 9, 12, …)*

Choose one:
- Gain **+1 Foundational Magical Effect** *(magical characters only)*
- Gain **+1 Class Mechanic Modifier**

Non-magical characters always gain the class modifier.

---

## Levels Divisible by 4
*(4, 8, 12, 16, …)*

- Gain **+1 Specialization**.

---

> **Note:** Some levels qualify for more than one benefit (e.g., level 12 is even, divisible by 3, and divisible by 4). Each benefit is granted separately.
