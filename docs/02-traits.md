# Traits

## Overview
There are 12 Core Traits divided into Physical and Mental categories. Each category contains 3 attack traits and 3 defense traits. Each trait has:
- A **value** — a static number added to rolls
- A **die** — rolled alongside the d20 (d4 through d12)

---

## The 12 Traits

### Physical Traits
| Type    | Trait     |
|---------|-----------|
| Attack  | Strength  |
| Attack  | Speed     |
| Attack  | Finesse   |
| Defense | Endurance |
| Defense | Balance   |
| Defense | Toughness |

### Mental Traits
| Type    | Trait     |
|---------|-----------|
| Attack  | Knowledge |
| Attack  | Wisdom    |
| Attack  | Charisma  |
| Defense | Willpower |
| Defense | Awareness |
| Defense | Empathy   |

---

## Trait Values

### At Character Creation
- Distribute **20 trait points** across the 12 traits.
- No more than **two traits** may begin at 4.
- No trait may begin **above 4**.
- Traits may begin at 0.

After level 1, trait values have no upper limit.

### Advancement
On **odd levels**, gain +1 trait point assignable to any trait (no cap after level 1).

---

## Trait Dice

### At Character Creation
All trait dice begin at **d4**.

### Advancement
On **even levels**, upgrade one trait die by one step:

**d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12**

Trait dice may never exceed d12.

---

## Using Traits
When a roll is required, the relevant trait is chosen based on player intent and GM adjudication.

### Attack Traits
All attacks must use one of the six attack traits:
- **Physical attacks:** Strength, Speed, or Finesse
- **Mental/Magical attacks:** Knowledge, Wisdom, or Charisma

The type of attack (physical vs. mental) is determined by the player's intent. This determines which defense is targeted and which mitigation applies.

---

## Derived Statistics

### Physical Defense
The target number an attacker must meet to land a physical hit.

**Physical Defense = 8 + Endurance + Balance + Level**

### Mental Defense
The target number an attacker must meet to land a mental or magical hit.

**Mental Defense = 8 + Willpower + Awareness + Level**

### Physical Mitigation
Reduces damage taken from physical hits. Does not apply to ongoing damage unless otherwise stated.

**Physical Mitigation = Toughness**

### Mental Mitigation
Reduces damage taken from mental or magical hits. Does not apply to ongoing damage unless otherwise stated.

**Mental Mitigation = Empathy**

### Resolve
Represents a character's ability to withstand harm, stress, and fatigue. Replaces traditional hit points.

**Resolve = (Endurance × Willpower) + Level**

> **Warning:** If either Endurance or Willpower is 0, Resolve equals 1 at level 1. A character with Resolve 1 will be taken out of combat by almost any hit. Investing nothing in these traits is a valid choice — but not a survivable one.

### Resonance
The magical resource used to enhance spells. Only relevant for magical characters.

**Resonance = Empathy + Balance + Level**

### Movement Speed
**Combat Speed:** 20 feet base, +5 feet per 2 points of Speed (rounded down).

| Speed | Combat Speed |
|-------|--------------|
| 0–1   | 20 ft        |
| 2–3   | 25 ft        |
| 4–5   | 30 ft        |
| 6–7   | 35 ft        |

**Out-of-combat speed** = double combat speed.
