# Magic

Magic is optional. A character chooses at character creation whether they use magic.

---

## Magical Domain
A Magical Domain defines the thematic space of a character's magic — a general description of the types of effects they can produce.

Players are **not** restricted to a spell list. Any effect that falls within the domain and is narratively justified may be attempted, subject to GM approval.

---

## Foundational Effects

### At Character Creation
A magical character defines **2 Foundational Effects**. These must:
- Fall within the chosen domain
- Be relatively minor
- Be approved by the GM
- Be narrow enough to prevent universal application, but broad enough to have multiple uses

Typically (but not required): one combat effect and one role-play effect.

### Rules
- Foundational effects cost **0 Resonance** at baseline.
- They define the character's core magical capabilities.
- Narrative use supersedes mechanical use.

> **Example:** A character who can hurl flame can light a campfire without spending Resonance.

### Healing Effects
Foundational effects that are used to restore Resolve must use at least 1 Resonance.

**Healing amount:** Trait Die + Trait Value. Each additional Resonance spent adds one Trait Die of the same size.

Mundane stabilization — bandages, splints, or similar physical first aid — follows the allied stabilization rules and costs no Resonance.

### Gaining More
Additional Foundational Effects are gained at every level divisible by 3 (3, 6, 9, 12, …).

---

## Spellcasting

1. Declare intent (including any Resonance upgrades).
2. GM assigns a Difficulty.
3. **Roll: d20 + Trait Die + Trait Value + Modifiers**

### Spell Damage
When a spell deals damage, the formula is:

**Trait Die + Trait Value + Modifiers − Mitigation**

Spells do not use a weapon die. At baseline, spell damage is lower than a physical attack — Resonance upgrades compensate for this.

### Spell Resolution

| Result                  | Outcome                                                                    |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Succeed by 5 or more    | Spend Resonance; refunded 1 Resonance after the spell resolves             |
| Succeed by less than 5  | Spend Resonance; spell works as intended                                   |
| Fail by 5 or less       | Spend Resonance; spell occurs but with an unintended result (GM determines)|
| Fail by more than 5     | Spend Resonance; spell backfires (GM determines)                           |

Resonance is always spent regardless of outcome.

### Casting from Resolve
If a character has **no Resonance remaining**, they may spend Resolve instead.
- This decision must be made **before** the spell is cast.
- A character may not cast a spell that would reduce their Resolve to 0 or below.

---

## Resonance

**Resonance = Empathy + Balance + Level**

Resonance is spent to enhance spells beyond their foundational baseline. Multiple Resonance may be spent on a single casting, and all selected upgrades apply simultaneously.

### Recovery
- **Rest** (once per adventuring day): Recover all Resonance.
- **Sleep** (end of adventuring day): Recover all Resonance.

---

## Resonance Upgrades

### Area
Each Resonance spent increases the diameter of the affected area by 5 feet.
*(1×1 → 2×2 → 3×3 → etc.)*

### Additional Targets
Each Resonance spent adds 1 additional target.

### Damage
Each Resonance spent adds 1 additional die to spell damage. The added die is always equal to the trait die used for the spell roll — so as the caster's trait die improves through advancement, each point of Resonance spent on damage becomes more powerful.

### Duration
By default, foundational spells resolve instantly. Each Resonance spent upgrades the duration of lasting effects by one step:

| Step | Duration                        |
|------|---------------------------------|
| 1    | Until end of target's next turn |
| 2    | 1 minute                        |
| 3    | 10 minutes                      |
| 4    | 1 hour                          |
| 5    | 1 day                           |
| 6    | 1 week                          |
| 7    | 1 month                         |
| 8    | 1 year                          |
| 9    | Permanent                       |

### Range
Each Resonance spent upgrades the spell to the next range band:

| Band | Range   |
|------|---------|
| 1    | Melee   |
| 2    | 15 ft   |
| 3    | 30 ft   |
| 4    | 60 ft   |

Further increases beyond 60 feet require GM discussion.

### Accuracy
| Cost        | Benefit                                   |
|-------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 1 Resonance | +2 to accuracy                            |
| 2 Resonance | Advantage (roll d20 twice, take higher)   |

### Speed (Casting Time Reduction)
Base casting time: **Major Action**.

If a spell would normally take longer (minutes, hours, days):
- Each Resonance spent moves it one step down the reversed Duration track until it reaches a Major Action.
- Spending 1 additional Resonance beyond that reduces the cast to a **Minor Adjustment**.

### Force (Forced Movement)
- If the spell **already moves a creature:** the first upgrade doubles movement distance.
- If the spell **does not move a creature:** the first upgrade adds 10 feet of movement.

Each additional application doubles the distance (10 → 20 → 40 → 80 → 160 ft, etc.).

Cost scales with target size:

| Target Size | Resonance Cost per Application |
|-------------|-------------------------------|
| 1×1         | 1                             |
| 2×2         | 2                             |
| 3×3         | 3                             |
| (continues) | +1 per size increment         |

### Permanence (Summoning)
| Cost        | Duration                   |
|-------------|----------------------------|
| 3 Resonance | Summon lasts for the battle |
| 5 Resonance | Summon lasts for the day    |

### Bypass Resistance
| Cost        | Effect              |
|-------------|---------------------|
| 2 Resonance | Bypass resistance   |
| 4 Resonance | Bypass immunity     |
